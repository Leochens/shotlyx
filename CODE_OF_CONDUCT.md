# Code of Conduct

Shotlyx should be a useful, respectful place to collaborate.

## Expected Behavior

- Be kind, direct, and constructive.
- Assume good intent, but accept correction when something lands poorly.
- Keep feedback focused on the work.
- Respect different experience levels, languages, locations, and backgrounds.
- Avoid sharing private user data, credentials, or private media.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, threats, personal attacks, or discriminatory language.
- Posting private information without permission.
- Repeated off-topic disruption.
- Abusive behavior in issues, pull requests, discussions, or community spaces.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, hide, or remove comments; close issues or pull requests; or block participation when needed to protect the project and its contributors.

For serious concerns, contact the maintainer privately through:

https://world.guantou.site/
